Aero Islands 1.1.1 by Isabel / nuclearobelisk is the generator's inspiration and
behavior reference. The owner reports rewriting the Java implementation.
Historical comparison fingerprints document selected algorithm correspondences;
they do not establish the authorship of the entire present Java generator.

The mod incorporates 160 byte-identical and 37 changed worldgen JSON resources
from the exact Aero Islands artifact Modrinth tGVwBATM / bzJ2edxy, whose embedded
descriptor declares GNU GPL v3.0+. Retained identifiers preserve seeded noise and
integrations; the external Aero mod and its custom items/blocks are not bundled.
One separately identified Minecraft resource is recorded in the same inventory.
Origins, changed-resource hashes and Java comparisons: metadata/worldgen_adoption.json
in the corresponding-source archive. No author endorsement is implied.

Additional ore-family and mining-tier tag files in the Minecraft namespace are
independently authored extensions. They are recorded as authored_resources in
that inventory, separately from incorporated Aero/Minecraft resources.

Local compatibility transformations were changed on 2026-10-31. Original code
reads CTOV, Dungeons and Taverns, Philips Ruins, Galosphere and Minecraft resources
from the user's installed originals. No repaired NBT templates or complete JSON
copies for these repairs are bundled. Their authors retain all rights in original
content; GPL licensing of this mod does not relicense the original resources.
data-patches.json contains addressable edits and wrappers;
metadata/runtime_data_patches.json and native_template_upgrades.json preserve pins.

Equipment artwork and compatibility
-----------------------------------
EquipmentModels.java and RodModels.java independently compose original pixel
materials and cuboid meshes for rods, grappling equipment and the lunch box.
The work is AI-assisted deterministic Java composition, without generative images,
and is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. Generated resources are distributed in the owned
atmosphere and Refined compatibility resource packs. Rod child models reference
the Refined Tools 3.0 parents for display transforms; no Refined cuboids or textures
are copied. A guarded runtime adapter removes stowed tackle from known cast models.
Upstream provider terms remain with their unchanged artifacts.
