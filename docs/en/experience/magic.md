Dedicated magic systems are planned. The aim is to make finding, studying and experimenting with spells a way to develop a character, with Otherworld D&D as the main reference. The mods and progression rules have not yet been selected.

## Learning and using magic

Several traditions can coexist when they offer different ways to play. Combat, healing, summoning, rituals, nature magic, necromancy and household uses are possible directions. A useful domestic spell can matter without being a strong weapon, and a spellcaster can also use weapons and peaceful skills.

Learning and casting should involve proportionate resources, preparation or limits. Mana, ingredients, cooldowns and rituals are possible tools; the combination remains open. Knowledge and investment should gradually expand what a character can do. Growing ingredients and preparing supplies can connect magic with [farming and cooking](../building-and-daily-life/food/).

## Travel

Learned flight and teleportation are intended alternatives to [aircraft](../engineering/). Their unlocks and use costs remain to be chosen; long or unlimited-duration flight can be appropriate after mastery.

Innate racial flight has a separate role and may be available from the beginning. The planned [character system](../character-and-combat/) should support different ways to travel without requiring every player to build a ship first.
