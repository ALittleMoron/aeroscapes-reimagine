[Iron’s Spells ’n Spellbooks](https://modrinth.com/mod/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/version/slKLosTb) adds spell scrolls, equipment and magical sites to explore. Village priest, temple and cartographer chests can contain a level-one Magic Missile or Healing Circle scroll.

## Your first spell

Mages start with **First magic mastery** already learned. Other characters can open **Talents** from the inventory, select Magic and spend their starting magic point on it. It teaches eight rank-1 spells without scrolls: Blood Needles, Magic Missile, Firebolt, Icicle, Ball Lightning, Shield, Heal and Oakskin. You gain attacks, personal healing and protection together; choose which to prepare rather than automatically filling every slot.

Mages receive the Flimsy Journal at character creation. To make another, use two copper ingots, one string and six paper. Equip it in the spell book accessory slot in Curios. The book is a required casting focus and keeps its equipment and reforging bonuses. All books use the same personal wheels, each with eight preparation slots.

Open **Prepared abilities** from the inventory or Curios. **Learned only** is on by default; turn it off to inspect the full catalogue. Unlearned and unavailable spells have a separate status, and an unsuccessful assignment explains the restriction. Search and the school filter work with either view. A known spell stays in the learned list while cooling down or when you lack mana or an equipped focus.

Dwarves and elves also see their innate ability here. It can use a slot in any wheel and works without a book or mana.

Select a wheel and a learned spell, then click a preparation slot to assign it. Right-click an occupied slot and click another to move its spell. **Clear selected slot** removes that assignment. Copies of the same spell in other wheels keep their slots and share its cooldown. You can change preparation during combat. Learned passive bonuses need no preparation slot. Hover over a slot for the assignment and casting controls.

Tap the **middle mouse button** to cast the selected prepared spell. Hold it to open the wheel. Press 1, 2 or 3 to switch wheels, then point at a slot and release to select it without casting. Releasing in the center keeps the new wheel’s last selection. The number keys select the hotbar as usual outside the wheel. With the default bindings, **Ctrl + middle mouse button** picks a block in Survival. In Creative, middle-click picks the targeted block or entity. Aim at empty space for the magic gesture, or assign separate cast and wheel keys in Controls. Optional keys for selecting each slot of the active wheel are also available; all extra keys are unbound by default. Selecting a slot never casts it.

The selected spell appears as an icon and name at the bottom left. An empty slot shows a short message for five seconds. Casting uses the spell's normal mana, reagent, cast-time and cooldown rules. Moving or clearing a slot and changing books do not reset its cooldown.

## Abilities from equipment

Rare versions of the Ring of Invisibility and Frostward Ring let you prepare **Invisibility I** and **Frostwave I** while wearing them, even without a book or talent. They cost mana and share cooldowns with their learned versions. Removing the ring takes its ability off the wheel and ends invisibility cast through the ring. Re-equipping restores the assignment if you have kept its slot.

An exceptional **Pearl Necklace III** grants **Stored Breath**. Equip it and breathe air for two seconds to charge it. Underwater, activate the prepared ability to restore enough air for about four seconds at the usual breathing rate. Aim at a nearby swimmer who needs air to give them the breath instead. The charge travels with the necklace, and each wearer needs to breathe air after using it before they can release another.

All three exceptional accessories can appear in expedition loot. An Invisibility Ring is a find. Hand crafting the Frostward Ring or Pearl Necklace III can produce an exceptional copy from the first attempt. The chance rises with your Crafting skill. Reforging the necklace keeps Stored Breath.

## One tree, four directions

The common first mastery leads into four functional directions. The nine native schools still determine school bonuses, resistances and equipment; they are not nine separate mastery chains.

Opening Magic centres the first mastery and the entrances to its four directions. **Overview** shows the whole tree. Search for a spell by its name and press Enter to jump to the talent that teaches it, including spells bundled into mastery. Select an unlearned talent to highlight the lowest-cost route from your current purchases. Its details show the cost of the remaining nodes including the goal, the shortfall in points and the required practice level.

| Direction                  | What you develop                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Destruction                | Direct attacks, projectiles, fields and channels, with choices for blast size and hits against Chilled or poisoned targets. |
| Restoration and Protection | Healing and protective spells, with improvements to their power, mana use, cooldowns and casting speed.                     |
| Summoning and Control      | Helpers, restraint and weakening, with power and resource improvements for this direction's spells.                         |
| Space and Transformation   | Travel, stealth, perception, digging and household changes, with more efficient and responsive casting.                     |

Masteries II, III, IV and V require Magic practice levels 5, 15, 30 and 45. Each stretch between masteries has one to three upgrades. From Destruction II to III you reduce the mana cost of attacks, then increase your mana capacity. From Restoration III to IV, first increase your mana capacity, then choose cheaper or faster casting, and finally shorten your cooldowns. The eight starting spells, 33 bundled spells and 70 individual unlocks cover all 111 spells.

Mana capacity, recovery, cooldown and casting-speed improvements sit inside the four directions. Summoning and Control starts with faster mana recovery for your whole repertoire. Individual spell talents sit beside their mastery. **Blast Geometry** follows Fireball and adds one block to its blast radius. **Winter Front** follows Blizzard and increases its radius by 15%. Casting-speed bonuses shorten ordinary preparation and extend continuous channels. Direct-hit bonuses against Chilled or poisoned targets apply outside persistent fields.

After mastery IV, two directions offer talents that change how you use familiar spells.

- **Guardian Aid.** Cast Fortify on a wounded ally, such as your tamed wolf. If the ward absorbs damage from a hostile creature, you have ten seconds to heal the same ally with Blessing of Life. Effective healing refunds a quarter of the mana spent on the heal, rounded down.
- **Binding Pact.** Restrain a target with Root. The first time your summoned helper damages it while those roots still hold, you recover a fifth of the mana spent on Root.

Both effects use prepared spells and require enough mana to pay for the cast up front. Magic has 131 one-point talents. Magic practice can earn every point in this tree. [Skills and talents](../character-and-combat/#skills-and-talents).

## Scrolls and personal ranks

Newly learned spells start at rank 1. Masteries I to V allow personal ranks up to 1, one quarter, one half, three quarters and the full native maximum, rounding fractions up. For a spell with maximum rank 10, the limits are 1, 3, 5, 8 and 10. Mastery raises the limit; it does not automatically raise a saved spell rank.

To improve a learned spell, craft an inscription table from a book, three wooden slabs and two wooden fences. Put a spell book in the upper input and a higher-rank scroll in the lower input, then press **Study scroll**. A successful improvement consumes exactly one scroll and raises your personal spell rank to the scroll's rank within its direction's mastery limit. The book remains a focus. A scroll of the same or a lower rank does nothing and is kept.

Permanent study rejects a scroll above the mastery limit intact. It does not learn a weaker version or store a future rank. The seven Eldritch spells also require their **Eldritch Manuscript** discovery for permanent use; their talent unlocks do not replace that discovery.

A scroll used in hand is a separate, consumable route. It needs no personal spell knowledge, talent, mastery, rank limit, discovery, focus or preparation slot, and casts without mana. Even an undiscovered Eldritch scroll can be consumed this way. It never teaches the spell or improves your saved rank. Native targeting and source rules still apply. Spells that require multiple activations, such as Portal, cannot be cast from a scroll. Scrolls cannot bypass travel limits such as the Angel Wings activation interval.

Pocket Dimension's personal room retains its exit frame, which returns its owner to the entry location. Leaving requires no mana, equipped focus or prepared spell.

Your learned ranks and preparation survive death, relogging and dimension changes. Changing books never teaches spells. Spells written into books under the earlier system are not converted into personal knowledge or refunded as talent points: learn their nodes and prepare them through the new menu.

When updating from the former school tree, its magic talent allocations are refunded once. Physical and profession allocations stay in place. Saved personal spell ranks and preparation slots are retained, but permanent casting requires relearning the corresponding unlocks in the new tree.

Characters updating the four-direction tree keep purchases, personal ranks and prepared slots. New links apply to future purchases. Unspent earned points reflect saved practice and the current limit for each pool.

## Travel

The **Amulet of Teleportation** goes in a necklace slot and requires total Ender Spell Power above 125%. At or below that threshold, the amulet falls to the ground. Three pieces of Shadowwalker armor raise the base 100% to 130%. While wearing the amulet, assign **Amulet Step** to one of the eight ability slots. Stand on solid ground, face the direction you want to travel and activate the step. It takes you up to 16 blocks and stops before walls or gaps. It costs no mana and requires no spellbook. Successful steps are 20 seconds apart, even if you remove and put the amulet back on.

The step and qualifying teleport spells also grant three seconds of Evasion I while the amulet is worn. Ender pearls do not trigger this effect. The item tooltip and EMI explain the conditions.

Teleport remains a short-distance spell with a maximum rank of 1 and a base cooldown of 20 seconds. Prepared Recall returns you to your respawn point with an equipped book focus; a Recall scroll offers one consumable use.

Angel Wings temporarily enables elytra-style flight. Its base duration rises from 10 to 50 seconds and its base mana cost from 80 to 160 with rank. Duration is capped at 50 seconds after all bonuses, with at least 120 seconds between activations.

Portal creates a temporary pair within one dimension, with at most 128 blocks between endpoints. Place both ends through its normal two-stage cast and recast controls. The initial cast has a base mana cost of 200 to 220. Pair lifetime is 20, 40 or 60 seconds by rank and cannot exceed 60 seconds after bonuses. At least 180 seconds must pass between completed pairs. Permanent portal-frame links are disabled.

Equipment, cooldown bonuses and scrolls cannot shorten the minimum intervals for Angel Wings or Portal. A paraglider lets you steer while descending, while [aircraft](../engineering/) can climb and carry supplies between islands.

## Alchemy

[Theurgy](https://modrinth.com/mod/theurgy/version/EBO0aQOY) adds an alchemical workshop for refining and transmuting materials. Craft **The Hermetica** from a book and two sand blocks for the apparatus layouts. EMI shows the inputs and outputs for each process.

Start with a sal ammoniac accumulator above its tank. Add water to the accumulator and draw the solvent from the tank with an empty bucket. Sal ammoniac crystals from new Overworld chunks speed up the process. The accumulator also works with water alone, so you can begin on an established island.

Use the solvent in a heated liquefaction cauldron to extract three units of iron sulfur from one raw iron. Calcine charcoal for mineral salt and distill cobblestone for mercury shards. Feed all three substances into the vessels beside a heated incubator to make iron ingots. The apparatus can also change one material into another using a sample of the target material. The book explains how to link the pedestals and power them with mercury flux.

For prospecting, sneak and right-click a suitable block with an attunable divination rod, then hold the use button to search for another block of that type nearby. A short press after a successful search shows the last result again without spending durability. Higher-tier rods accept more types of ore.

## Casting your own patterns

Hex Casting lets any character compose spells with a staff. Mine a mature amethyst cluster with a pickaxe without Silk Touch to collect dust and occasionally charged amethyst. Combine a book with the dust for the field guide. Three sticks, an oak plank and charged amethyst make an oak staff. Keep some dust for casting. [HexCast NEOFORGED](https://modrinth.com/mod/hexcast-neoforged/version/3qkLQwpf)

For a first light, stand in an empty space and draw **Mind's Reflection**, **Compass' Purification II**, then **Conjure Light**. The first pattern selects the caster, the second finds the position of their feet and the third places a light there for one dust. The guide shows the strokes for each pattern. Hex uses its own media and pattern knowledge. Characters can begin at any point in the talent tree.

A **Cypher**, **Trinket** or **Artifact** can hold a program. Use the guide's instructions to write it into the item, then open **Prepared Abilities** and assign that item to a slot in any wheel. Keep it on your hotbar or in your offhand. Holding the middle mouse button selects a slot in the wheel. A short press uses the selected item. A hotbar item moves into your main hand for the cast. Renaming the items at an anvil helps distinguish programs of the same type.

The wheel spends the item's native charge. A cypher breaks when spent, a trinket can recharge, and an artifact can also draw media from inventory. Moving or recharging the item keeps its assignment. If you put it in a chest, the slot shows it as missing until you recover it.

Blink reaches 16 blocks and Greater Teleport reaches 64. Impulse vectors can have length up to 1.5, and levitation lasts up to four seconds. The same target can receive one Hex movement effect every 20 seconds, including Altiora. The two patterns that grant free flight and the pattern that changes body scale are disabled. Status effects accept up to level II where potency is adjustable, and explosions accept up to strength 4. Staff casting, stored programs and circles use the same limits.
