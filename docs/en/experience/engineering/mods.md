The engineering group uses Create for mechanical work and Aeronautics for aircraft. Food production, useful mechanisms and building details connect that machinery with everyday activities.

## Workshops and transport

[Create](https://modrinth.com/project/LNytGWDc) supplies mechanical power, processing machines, moving contraptions, item transport and trains. Players combine these parts into a workshop or transport route suited to the site. Its in-game Ponder demonstrations explain individual components.

[Bits ’n’ Bobs](https://modrinth.com/project/T8bvmqVZ) adds mechanical and decorative pieces in the same style. Tile variants, chairs, lamps, girders and larger displays help furnish the spaces around the machinery. The workshop can therefore share its materials and details with a station, factory or ship interior.

[Create: Connected](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-connected) adds compact transmissions, kinetic batteries and horizontal tanks and vaults. A battery stores spare mechanical power and feeds it back into the network as demand rises.

[RSGauges Unofficial Fix](https://modrinth.com/mod/rsgauges-unofficial-fix) makes redstone circuits easier to read and operate. Gauges show the signal strength, while timers, switches and detectors can control alarms or machinery.

[Create: Diesel Generators](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-diesel-generators/version/UoPH8lO1) adds crop-based fuels, finite oil wells, refining and compact, modular and huge engines. [Create: Liquid Fuel](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-liquid-fuel/version/rLn2bDVE) feeds these fuels and lava into ordinary Blaze Burners. [Engines, fuel and oil](../fuel/) explains production, prospecting, field lifetimes and heat.

## Mechanical processing and foundry

[Vintage Improvements — SSW Edition](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/vintage-improvenents-ssw-edition) adds bending, belt grinding, spring coiling, a helve hammer, a centrifuge and a vibrating table. They form parts, polish surfaces, separate mixtures and unpack suitable reversible recipes. Its stationary processing laser remains available. The diamond-to-emerald joke and the lathe's cobblestone-to-gravel test recipe are removed.

The lathe makes shaped press heads, while the vacuum chamber supports vacuum and pressure recipes, including sulfur processing.

[Create: Metallurgy](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-metallurgy) adds a foundry basin and mixer, graphite molds, faucets and casting tables and basins. Start with andesite alloy and refractory mortar; graphite is made from coal or charcoal and clay. Press graphite into a blank mold, then use a stonecutter to select a mold. Ordinary metal casting reuses the mold. Recipes and Ponder show the individual assemblies.

Melt iron, then mix 270 mB of molten iron with one coke to obtain 270 mB of steel at ordinary heat. Coal can be smelted into coke. Existing artillery steel production remains available too. Either provider's steel ingot can be pressed into one steel sheet. The foundry also casts and remelts that sheet for 90 mB. Rods and wires each represent 45 mB: two rods make two wires, so recycling steel, bronze, zinc or netherite does not multiply the metal.

Wolframite occurs in Nether netherrack and requires a diamond-tier pickaxe. Superheat is needed to melt tungsten and to alloy it with andesite alloy into obdurium, which opens the industrial crucible. In existing worlds, look in newly generated Nether chunks for the new ore. Ordinary furnaces and the basic foundry remain useful before that upgrade.

Ladles carry up to 4000 mB and can be placed, thrown or sent through logistics. Manual opening releases up to 1000 mB and has a native 10% chance of briefly igniting the holder, even with cold fluid or an empty ladle. Steel-based reforging reduces that opening risk to 7.5%, 5%, 2.5% and 0% at Fine, Exceptional, Epic and Legendary quality. Capacity and fluid quantities stay unchanged; other fire sources still apply. EMI lists the materials, odds and effects for each style.

## Artillery

[Create Big Cannons](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-big-cannons) lets players cast and machine cannon parts, assemble them on a mount, and load them with a ram rod or a mechanical loader. Choose the projectile and fuze for the target. Artillery can break terrain and destroy items caught in a blast. Projectiles can temporarily load chunks during flight.

## Food production

The cooking additions let a farm and kitchen join the workshop's supply network: machinery processes ingredients, while transport carries prepared food to storage or a loading point. [Cooking and farming](../../building-and-daily-life/food/) explains the roles of these mods.

## Aircraft equipment

[Aeronautics](https://modrinth.com/project/oWaK0Q19) uses Sable for its physical contraptions. Being able to place a machine on a ship does not establish that it works while moving.

[Gyro Stabilizers](https://modrinth.com/project/JcEFpckH) adds a gyroscope. [Throwable Rope Connector](https://modrinth.com/project/9xiCbKmM) allows rope connectors to be thrown, launched or fired from a mount for docking. These additions support building and handling a ship within Aeronautics’ own system.

Shared container access and portable supplies are described under [convenience](../../quality-of-life/). The intended role of ships and alternative ways to travel are covered in [engineering and air travel](../).
