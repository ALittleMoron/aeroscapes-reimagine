Food connects a garden, kitchen and workshop. The included mods expand ingredients and preparation methods, while Create offers ways to turn some of that work into production lines. Ordinary hunger and saturation remain the current eating mechanics; nutrition groups and thirst are planned.

## Ingredients and kitchens

[Farmer’s Delight](https://modrinth.com/project/R2OftAxM) adds crops such as cabbage, tomatoes, onions and rice, with a cutting board, cooking pot, skillet and stove for the kitchen. Cabinets and produce crates also connect food preparation with furnishing the room.

Nature’s Delight brings Farmer’s Delight together with Nature’s Spirit. Fungi Delight adds mushrooms and food, while Brewin’ and Chewin’ adds fermentation. These connections give ingredients from the wider world uses at home and offer different kinds of kitchen projects.

Crate Delight adds crates and bags for produce. Crops Love Rain makes crops grow faster in rain, so weather also has a practical place in farming.

## Cooking with machinery

[Create: Food](https://modrinth.com/project/4HnO3el1) expands food content shared with Create and Farmer’s Delight. [Delightful Creators](https://modrinth.com/project/jmJ87gsb) connects Farmer’s Delight preparation with Create automation. Ratatouille supplies further farming and processing equipment, including an oven, thresher, irrigation tower and compost tower.

A kitchen can remain a small place for manual cooking or become part of an automated farm. Individual recipes and uses can be browsed through [EMI](../../quality-of-life/); the wider production group is described under [machines and logistics](../../engineering/mods/).

## Planned nutrition and thirst

The intended nutrition system will encourage food variety through food groups. A mild temporary weakness is the preferred consequence of a low group, ending when nutrition recovers. Groups, thresholds and exact effects have not yet been selected. This will accompany ordinary hunger.

Planned thirst includes water quality. Untreated water may be drunk with a negative effect; boiling or another supported purification method should prepare normal drinking water. Consumption should be moderate. Prolonged dehydration may have stronger consequences, potentially including periodic damage, with timing and severity still to be decided.
