Minecraft villages and [ChoiceTheorem’s Overhauled Village](https://modrinth.com/project/fgmhI8kH) provide settlements to discover in the island world. Vanilla villagers and trading remain available, including a trading hall organized by the player.

<Screenshot id="village" eager />

Settlement development and resident errands are planned additions. The settlement and quest systems are still being selected.

## Player colonies

Players should see their contribution in new buildings and upgrades funded by the resources they supply. Useful resident work, conversations and relationships will complement construction and supply. The exact roles and building methods will depend on the selected system.

Available land, resources and routes between buildings should shape a settlement. Players may establish colonies on different islands. Founding a colony remains optional alongside [engineering](../engineering/), [building](../building-and-daily-life/) and exploration.

Raids, resident deaths, damage and repairs may be part of colony life within the mod’s native capabilities. Generated inhabited and abandoned colonies are also desired places to discover; their implementation remains open.

## Local quests

NPC quests should offer activities through residents: take a task, track it and receive a reward on completion. Everyday errands, including repeatable tasks, are planned for `1.0.0` and can give an expedition another purpose. Story chains are currently left for later development.

More trade between islands, connections to factories, additional building styles and expedition companions are possible later developments. Traveling with companions aboard ships is an idea for the future.
