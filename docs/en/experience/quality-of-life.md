The convenience tools help players find information, organize belongings and remember routes. They are shared by building, cooking, engineering and exploration.

## Recipes and information

[EMI](https://modrinth.com/project/fRiHVvU7) lets players browse recipes and item uses across the pack. Jade shows information about the block or entity being looked at, while Item Descriptions adds descriptions of items, blocks and creatures. AppleSkin displays food information for hunger and saturation.

[JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) supplies additional processing, cooking and cutting recipes through the same EMI interface. [Advanced Loot Info](https://modrinth.com/mod/advanced-loot-info), with [Advanced Core Info](https://modrinth.com/mod/advanced-core-info), adds block, creature and chest loot with conditions and functions. Conditional values describe the configured rules; they are not an unconditional probability of finding an item on an island. Villager trade pages remain supplied by the existing trade viewer.

[Jade Addons](https://modrinth.com/mod/jade-addons-forge) expands the existing block information with supported machine and contraption details, including fuel and carried inventories. The integrated engineering-goggles display remains disabled by default.

[EMI Enchanting](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi-enchanting) adds enchantment information pages to [EMI](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi), and [EMIffect](https://modrinth.com/mod/emiffect) adds status-effect information. [Enchantment Descriptions](https://modrinth.com/mod/enchantment-descriptions) also explains enchantments directly in book and equipment tooltips. Missing descriptions for the pack's enchantments and effects are supplied in English and Russian.

[Smart Tooltip Scroll](https://modrinth.com/mod/smart-tooltip-scroll) keeps oversized tooltips readable through resizing and scrolling. [Equipment Compare](https://modrinth.com/mod/equipment-compare) shows comparison tooltips while holding Shift over equipment. [Legendary Tooltips](https://modrinth.com/mod/legendary-tooltips) adds decorative frames; its separate scrolling feature stays disabled by default. Their settings are available through the mod list. Item model previews are disabled in the starting profile because they overlapped [Quark](https://modrinth.com/mod/quark) attribute icons. Model previews remain configurable in the mod list.

[Polymorph](https://modrinth.com/mod/polymorph) adds a result-selection button when the same crafting ingredients match several recipes. Choose the intended item beside the output slot; its EMI integration also supports selecting a recipe while transferring ingredients. For example, beetroot can produce red dye or Create Food's shredded beetroot. This crafting-screen choice does not set the output of Create's mechanical crafters.

[Visual Workbench](https://modrinth.com/mod/visual-workbench) keeps crafting ingredients in the table when its menu closes and displays them on top. The table's contents belong to that block, so return to the same table to continue a craft.

[Crafting Tweaks](https://modrinth.com/mod/crafting-tweaks) adds controls to rotate, distribute and return crafting ingredients. The crafting grid in [Tom's Simple Storage](https://modrinth.com/mod/toms-storage) also supports these operations. Sorting remains available through [Inventory Profiles Next](https://modrinth.com/mod/inventory-profiles-next).

[EMI: Create Schematics](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi-create-schematics) adds an import button to [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create)'s clipboard. It puts unchecked material entries and their quantities into EMI favorites. Importing a new list replaces the helper's previous material entries while preserving ordinary favorites.

[Beautiful Enchanted Books](https://modrinth.com/mod/beautiful-enchanted-books-mod-edition) gives supported enchantments distinct book appearances. A book with several enchantments uses the first stored enchantment for its appearance; unsupported entries retain the ordinary book model.

Shulker Box Tooltip previews shulker-box contents. Effect Timer Plus adds a countdown and potency indicator to status-effect icons.

[EMI Trades Reborn](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi-trades-reborn) adds villager and wandering trader recipes to [EMI](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi), with profession, level and base prices. A villager's actual price can differ because of discounts and demand. Random and special modded offers may appear as individual examples; check the trader's actual offers. On a remote server, server changes to trades may be absent from the client reference.

## Inventory and storage

[Inventory Profiles Next](https://modrinth.com/project/O7RBXm3n) provides sorting and automatic refill, while Mouse Tweaks expands mouse-based inventory handling. Refill arrow overlays on inventory and hotbar slots are hidden by default.

[InvMove](https://modrinth.com/mod/invmove) lets players move while inventory menus are open. Movement stops while the EMI search field has focus and while holding the tutorial key over a [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create) Ponder item. Configuration and schematic toolkit menus keep movement disabled while editing settings or plans.

Sophisticated Backpacks and Sophisticated Storage provide portable and stationary containers. [Tom’s Simple Storage](https://modrinth.com/project/XZNI4Cpy) adds a common terminal for connected physical containers. Recipes for both wireless terminals are disabled, so this does not provide distant wireless access.

[Create Style Sophisticated Backpacks](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/create-sophisticated-backpacks) changes the existing backpacks and supported upgrade icons to match Create's visual style. It is an optional resourcepack; backpack storage and upgrade mechanics remain the same.

Carry On helps move supported blocks. Ordinary and trial spawners are excluded, and the mod’s native restrictions still apply to other blocks.

[Pick Up Notifier](https://modrinth.com/mod/pick-up-notifier) briefly shows the names and quantities of collected items, including supported backpack pickups. [Clumps](https://modrinth.com/mod/clumps) merges nearby experience orbs; collected experience still repairs equipment with Mending and fills the experience bar.

## Anvils and enchanting

[Easy Anvils](https://modrinth.com/mod/easy-anvils) keeps inputs in the anvil and displays them on the block. Renaming on its own is free and does not damage the anvil. Repairs and combining enchantments retain ordinary material costs, experience costs and increasing prior-work penalties. Combined operations and special recipes retain their existing pricing. Operations above 40 levels remain available at their full price. Iron blocks can repair a worn anvil, and name tags can be edited while crouching without an anvil.

[Easy Magic](https://modrinth.com/mod/easy-magic) keeps the item and lapis in the enchanting table after closing its menu. A manual reroll costs one lapis and five experience points. Ordinary bookshelf placement and the 15-power requirement remain; each offer reveals one enchantment. The extra chiseled-bookshelf overlay is disabled because the existing block-information display already shows its books.

If an anvil or enchanting table in an existing world no longer opens after adding these mods, break and replace it. Placing the ordinary block item creates the version with a persistent inventory.

## Maps and recovery

Xaero’s World Map, Minimap and personal waypoints help record the landscape and mark places to revisit. Creature radar is disabled. In survival without cheats, waypoint teleportation is unavailable; a marker helps navigation while the journey remains part of play.

Spyglass Improvements adds adjustable zoom, quick access and spyglass overlays.

[Nature’s Compass](https://modrinth.com/mod/natures-compass) locates biomes, and [Explorer’s Compass](https://modrinth.com/mod/explorers-compass) locates structures. Both search for free within 5,000 blocks of the search origin. Extended search requires one available experience level to start, reaches 10,000 blocks and charges it only when a result beyond 5,000 blocks is found. Failed or cancelled searches are free. A search that reaches its sample limit is incomplete; a biome result can also miss small patches and does not guarantee a dry landing place.

Corpse provides inventory recovery through a body after death. Recovery after a fall into the void and interactions with moving ships remain unresolved.

[Simple Backups](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-backups) saves a full world copy after each hour of active play, without routine start and finish messages. Up to five copies per world share a soft 2 GiB storage budget. The newest complete copy of every world is kept even if those minimum copies exceed the budget. Compression is light, and creating the snapshot can pause play while copying saved files and needs temporary space for an uncompressed copy. The distant-terrain cache is regenerated after restoration. Unrelated and older unmanaged archives remain untouched.

Backups are ZIP files in `simplebackups`, beside the instance's `config` and `mods` folders. To restore one, close the game or server, preserve the current world separately and extract the archived world folder into a new save folder with the same pack installed. Check the restored save before replacing the original.

## Riding

[Horseman](https://modrinth.com/mod/horseman) adds controlled swimming, smoother stepping and jumping, hitching, inventory switching and copper-horn recall across dimensions. Its movement, taming, cargo and camera conveniences are enabled, including faster two-block step-downs and taming with an item in hand. Mounted mining assistance applies to horses, donkeys, mules, llamas and camels. The existing mount bars provide the HUD. Recall preserves the living animal and its current cargo; it does not resurrect a dead mount.

## Building from schematics

[Forgematica](https://modrinth.com/mod/forgematica), with [MaFgLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/mafglib), supplies schematic projections, material lists and a verifier that compares a plan with placed blocks. Open its menu with Right Alt, load a schematic and create a placement; Right Alt + L opens its material list. These shortcuts leave the world map's M key available.

The starting configuration keeps ordinary block reach and leaves Easy Place disabled. Gather the listed materials and place blocks yourself. Projections use world coordinates; alignment with moving ships is not established.

[Accurate Block Placement Reborn](https://modrinth.com/mod/accurate-block-placement-reborn) makes held-button block placement follow each targeted space without the ordinary placement delay. It uses the existing compatibility bridge. Accurate placement is enabled; fast breaking is disabled. Interacting with blocks and using items keeps its ordinary behavior, and placement retains ordinary reach and material consumption.

## Controls and presentation

[BetterF3](https://modrinth.com/mod/betterf3) groups and colors the information on the F3 debug screen. Its settings let you choose which sections to show.

[Cherished Worlds](https://modrinth.com/mod/cherished-worlds) pins favorite worlds and servers at the top of their lists. A favorite world must be unmarked before it can be deleted; this protection complements keeping world backups.

[Leave My Bars Alone](https://modrinth.com/mod/leave-my-bars-alone) keeps hunger visible beside mount health and shows experience while the jump bar is idle.

[Better Advancements](https://modrinth.com/mod/better-advancements) expands the advancement screen, while [Advancement Plaques](https://modrinth.com/mod/advancement-plaques) replaces ordinary advancement notifications with decorated plaques. As in vanilla Minecraft, the screen stays empty until the first advancement is earned. The mod list provides screen, appearance and notification sound settings.

[Boat Item View](https://modrinth.com/mod/boat-item-view) keeps held maps visible while rowing an ordinary boat. Crouch and right-click a boat or minecart to eject its mob passengers with [Dismount Entity](https://modrinth.com/mod/dismount-entity). Hold an item if [Carry On](https://modrinth.com/mod/carry-on) intercepts the interaction for carrying. Player passengers stay seated by default.

[Trade Cycling](https://modrinth.com/mod/trade-cycling) adds a button to reroll a villager's offers before the first trade. Trading locks the offers.

Controlling adds search to the keybinding menu, helping players find the controls supplied by different mods. Items lying flat on the ground and deliberate pickup by right-click or crouching are desired future features; they are not currently supplied. Which resources should still be collected automatically remains to be selected.
