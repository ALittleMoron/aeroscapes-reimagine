The Overworld is a landscape of floating islands: forests, fields, deserts and rocky heights separated by open space. Caves run through the islands, while villages, ruins and dungeons give journeys destinations.

## Distance and height

An island can be close enough to reach with a bridge or stand across a wide gap. Different heights make the route matter too: reaching lower ground can be easier than returning home. The terrain gives materials, preparation and transport a practical purpose.

[Engineering and air travel](../engineering/) offers ways to scout, carry supplies and connect distant places. Building a bridge remains another choice. Future [character abilities](../character-and-combat/) and [magic](../magic/) will give players more ways to approach the same landscape.

## Discoveries and resources

An expedition can bring back plants for a garden, materials for a building, equipment or supplies for a workshop. Villages and small ruins can be worth a stop on the way to a larger adventure. The [places and discoveries](discoveries/) page describes the landscapes and authored places already included.

What is nearby depends on the world. The starting island's biome, resources and dangers remain random. A quiet forest, a useful deposit or an attractive place to build can shape the next few sessions as much as a dungeon.

## Building on islands

An untouched island is also a destination. A small cabin, a farm or a workshop can grow into a long project in its own right. [Building and daily life](../building-and-daily-life/) connects the materials found on journeys with kitchens, gardens and homes.

## The Nether and End

The Nether and End provide other places to explore while the island Overworld remains the center of the pack. Their terrain keeps its familiar character, with additional structures already supplied by Dungeons and Taverns. Further development is planned through new biomes, creatures and activities.

## Further development

The intended balance leaves natural space between discoveries. Medium islands should be the most common, small ones frequent and large ones less common, with exceptionally rare expanses large enough for a city or several settlements. Island sizes, distances and the frequency of conspicuous buildings still need refinement.

Resource specialization by biome and height is a future balancing direction. Common necessities such as coal and iron should be widespread across the world, with different islands offering reasons to travel. Exact resource distributions and dungeon difficulty by height have not been established.

[Settlements and NPC quests](../settlements/) are also planned, alongside the wider character and magic systems. They will add activities to the world and reasons to return to particular places.

[How the island world works](generation/) explains the present terrain, starting-island selection and placement of buildings and underground places.
