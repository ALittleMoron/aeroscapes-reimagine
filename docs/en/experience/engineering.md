[Create](https://modrinth.com/project/LNytGWDc) and [Aeronautics](https://modrinth.com/project/oWaK0Q19) connect engineering with travel. A workshop processes materials and supplies a home; an aircraft carries materials and finished goods to another island. Players design the arrangement of machines and the shape of their vehicles.

## Machines and production

Engineering centers on Create’s moving parts, mechanical power and item handling. Farms, material processing, kitchens and transport routes give machinery different jobs. A factory can also be a building project, with its layout chosen for the place and the player’s interests.

[Machines and logistics](mods/) explains the roles of the engineering additions; [cooking and farming](../building-and-daily-life/food/) covers kitchen automation.

## Aircraft

Aeronautics adds vehicles and physical contraptions using Sable. Ships are intended for scouting, carrying resources and supplying distant homes, with room for designs ranging from small scouts to cargo airships and flying homes.

The included additions provide gyroscopic stabilization and rope connectors for docking. Aeronautics’ native mechanics remain the starting point. Shipbuilding carries consequences: a mistake may cost a vessel, and recovering a character’s inventory does not recover the ship.

A home aboard ship is part of the intended experience. The range of storage, kitchen equipment and machinery usable while moving is still being established; not every stationary machine can be assumed to work on a vessel.

## Travel choices

The first useful aircraft should make island travel easier after the effort of building it. Experimenting with a vessel should lead to more comfortable journeys, more capable expeditions, cargo transport and life aboard, supporting the player's other projects. Bridges and player-built routes remain options; learned [magic](../magic/) may provide fitting alternatives through its own progression. There is no compulsory transport sequence.

Elytra are planned to be disabled, with a valuable reward that gives no strong flight advantage replacing them in item frames aboard End ships. This change has not been implemented; the replacement and treatment of existing items remain to be chosen. The reward should be proportionate to the expedition and the End's other useful finds. The aim is to preserve reasons to build and improve aircraft while keeping the End worth exploring. Planned [races](../character-and-combat/#races-and-classes) will also avoid unrestricted flight from the start.
