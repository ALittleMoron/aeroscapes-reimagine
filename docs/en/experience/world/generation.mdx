The floating Overworld is based on [Aero Islands](https://modrinth.com/mod/tGVwBATM) generation. Reimagine’s own mod develops that terrain alongside other mods’ biomes and structures: moving them to islands exposed assumptions about continuous land. The Default world preset is currently supported.

[C2ME](https://modrinth.com/mod/COlSi5iR) distributes chunk loading and generation across CPU threads. OpenCL can use a compatible GPU for part of terrain generation; when that acceleration is unavailable, generation uses the CPU. [Structure Layout Optimizer](https://modrinth.com/mod/ayPU0OHc) reduces work when assembling and placing structures.

If new terrain struggles to keep up while flying, compare GPU and CPU generation with your usual graphics settings. The faster option depends on your hardware; the pack does not select it automatically.

To try CPU generation, close the game, set `enabled = false` under `[openclAccel]` in `config/c2me.toml`, then restart. Set it back to `true` to try GPU generation again. Compare both terrain arrival and the smoothness of movement in new areas.

Feature placement is prepared when the world opens. In singleplayer, ordinary terrain chunks arrive in batches adjusted to the client’s processing speed, spreading the work of receiving a large ready area.

## Islands, mountains and lower heights

Most islands used to be concentrated high above a largely empty lower world. Lower islands and additional ore placements were added so exploration could extend downward. The Overworld now spans Y−128…383; the original upper islands retain their height range.

The added large islands initially looked too smooth, and their mountains resembled symmetrical cones. Their terrain was reworked into connected areas at different heights, with hills, ridges, chasms and caves. One such island can contain several biomes, with plains alongside high peaks. These large formations are rare.

Small islands are much less frequent and vary more in size and position, reducing the repeating grid of similar shapes between larger landforms. Some small and medium islands have one surface biome throughout their body; the largest formations retain several biomes. Ocean basins and mountain habitats keep their own biome selection.

High islands previously often lost the long cave passages because cave generation selected an empty biome below them or started outside the island; several bulk rock types also could not be carved. Cave passages and chambers now start inside the island layers; small noise pockets no longer stand in for cave systems. Carving protects the floors and rims of water and lava basins. Granite and diorite deposits are placed below the actual island surface rather than at a random height that often fell in empty space.

Badlands spires that erroneously extended through the void to the bottom of the world were corrected; those growing on the islands remain. Long accidental basalt bridges between islands separated by large height gaps were also removed.

## Low volcanic islands

Occasional broad volcanic islands contain lava below Y0. Their enclosed basins have blackstone and deepslate shores with magma patches, cooled basalt surfaces and groups of basalt columns, providing a concentrated lava source without adding more high-altitude lava lakes. They use their own volcanic biome with no Nether structures or creature spawns of its own.

## Oceans

A map could previously show an ocean where there was only void. Ocean regions now have a chance to contain large islands with deep basins, uneven seabeds, bays and shores. Ocean and shore biomes follow these basins, while monument, shipwreck and other water-structure placement adapts to the local waterline. These are separate island basins rather than a continuous sea beneath the islands.

[Terralith](https://modrinth.com/datapack/8oi3bsk5), [Nature’s Spirit](https://modrinth.com/mod/WBvnqHfV) and [Galosphere](https://modrinth.com/mod/Kn7xyQ1v) provide the other landscapes. Aero’s four original biomes and its custom content are excluded. Basalt formations and geodes were converted to vanilla materials, while the project’s own fractured jungle is retained. [Places and discoveries](../discoveries/) describes the landscapes and things to find.

## Biomes and empty space

Previously, biome searches could point into an empty gap because the climate map assigned ordinary biomes there. Empty space away from island terrain now uses the Open Sky biome, which has no vegetation, structures or creature spawns of its own. A small buffer around islands retains their biomes, including their cave interiors; a search can therefore end just outside a cliff rather than exactly on a solid block.

Open air between islands follows the world's rain and thunderstorm weather, including at flight heights.

Biome names are provided in English and Russian.

In blackstone terrain such as Mantle Caves, ore generation previously left deepslate patches around deposits. Those deposits now use blackstone variants for the eight vanilla ores, [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create)'s zinc and [Galosphere](https://modrinth.com/mod/galosphere)'s palladium. Their mining requirements, drops and processing follow the corresponding deepslate ores. Silk Touch preserves the blackstone ore block.

A compatibility repair also restores [Quark](https://quarkmod.net/)'s cave biome to island generation after it had been skipped by the biome source.

## How structure placement was repaired

Structure mods expected continuous land: on floating islands, parts of dungeons lacked support and large complexes often hung outside the terrain. Placement was reworked to account for the size and support of the whole complex. The pack tries to fit it near the original starting point and rejects the attempt if no suitable site is available. Individual passages and entrances can emerge from slopes; intended aerial structures and open bridges are retained.

Trial chambers and the pink salt shrine from [Galosphere](https://modrinth.com/mod/Kn7xyQ1v) could previously expose large rooms outside an island. The pack now selects sites with rock above most exterior roofs, counting large rooms by their area. Ocean Fortress from [Philips Ruins](https://modrinth.com/mod/KdJhOYVV) also checks ground beneath its whole foundation, since support under its center alone allowed it to hang over an island edge. Terrain filling used to block the spaces between ancient city buildings; they now share a cavern with uneven ceilings. Sites where a city would cut through an ocean basin’s floor and send water into the cavern are also rejected.

The stricter checks have made some structures rarer. A matching biome alone does not guarantee that a particular island can accommodate a dungeon.

Shipwrecks also check the ground at their final placement height: averaging an upper island and a much lower seabed could previously leave a hull hanging between them. The badlands miner outpost from [Dungeons and Taverns](https://modrinth.com/mod/dungeons-and-taverns) requires natural ground beneath its full main platform, so its square template no longer qualifies as an island by itself.

Creeping Crypt from [Dungeons and Taverns](https://modrinth.com/mod/dungeons-and-taverns) previously checked the ground beneath its entrance alone, allowing most underground rooms to hang outside an island. Its placement now checks the underground assembly's support and rock cover while retaining the surface entrance, trying nearby ground before rejecting an unsuitable site.

Buried treasure could register a location without a chest on basalt shores because the usual search did not accept that rock. Newly generated treasures retry the native search on basalt only after the usual search fails, retaining their normal coordinates and loot.

## Starting a game and saved worlds

New worlds use a separately selected natural starting island; its biome, resources and nearby dangers remain random. An existing world’s starting point is not moved.

Create a new world to see the complete terrain changes. Explored chunks are not rebuilt, and their boundaries with new terrain can be abrupt. Saves containing removed Aero content have no supported conversion. Back up the world before updating; rollback requires that backup and the previous pack. See [Compatibility and support](../../../known-issues/#saved-worlds) for details.
