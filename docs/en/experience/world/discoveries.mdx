An expedition can lead to a new landscape, a village, an excavation or a large dungeon. Wildlife, wetland ruins and inhabited vessels give journeys smaller encounters along the way. This page describes how these discoveries fit together; the [pack contents](../../../reference/mods/) page lists the selected files.

## Landscapes and materials

[Terralith](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith) and [Nature’s Spirit](https://modrinth.com/mod/natures-spirit) give the islands much of their surface variety. Forests, flowered fields, dry regions, snowy landscapes and unusual rocky places make different islands recognizable. The selected [Terralith](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith) landscapes include lavender valleys, sakura groves, deserts and oases, as well as volcanic and alpine regions.

[Nature’s Spirit](https://modrinth.com/mod/natures-spirit) also adds trees and building materials, including maple, redwood and wisteria wood, chert and travertine. Bringing home a plant or a new material can be an expedition’s reward. These finds connect exploration with [gardens, cooking and building](../../building-and-daily-life/).

[Custom Biome Saplings](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith-biome-saplings) lets suitable saplings grow trees associated with their biome. It carries some of that regional character into growing and tending trees.

[This Rocks!](https://modrinth.com/mod/this-rocks-forge) adds loose stones, sticks, pinecones and coastal debris. These details can appear on exposed lower islands as well as the highest surface, with shells and starfish on basin floors. Collecting debris supplies small amounts of ordinary materials; stone fragments also have crafting recipes.

## Villages and everyday places

[ChoiceTheorem’s Overhauled Village](https://modrinth.com/mod/ct-overhaul-village) adds villages and outposts in different sizes and regional styles. [Nature’s Spirit](https://modrinth.com/mod/natures-spirit) contributes cypress, wisteria, adobe and coconut villages. These are places to encounter while traveling; the planned player-developed colony system has its own role in [settlements](../../settlements/).

[Dungeons and Taverns](https://modrinth.com/datapack/dungeons-and-taverns) adds smaller scenes alongside its larger adventures: taverns, camps, wells, graveyards and remnants of earlier buildings. [Terralith](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith) contributes ruins and small places such as huts, lodges and outposts. A modest discovery can give an island a landmark without filling it with a large dungeon.

[Sky Villages](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sky-villages-forge) adds rare settlements high in the sky. Their houses, platforms and bridges form a complete village below the world's upper boundary. Reaching the settlement and finding room to land become part of an aerial expedition.

## Vessels in the sky

[Creat Aeronautics Structures](https://modrinth.com/mod/creat-aeronautics-structures/version/aMCksPon) supplies four intact aerial encounters: merchant and pillager balloons and dirigibles. Merchant decks offer trading stops; pillager crews make the other pair hostile destinations with loot to search. These vessels are static structures. Plan the approach to their decks and the return journey as you would for another island destination.

## Ruins and larger adventures

[Explorify](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/explorify) adds campsites, farmsteads, watchtowers, supply caches and shrines. These give shorter journeys places to stop alongside the larger dungeons. Their foundations and connecting paths must fit the island terrain.

[Philips Ruins](https://modrinth.com/mod/philips-ruins) adds weathered remains, towers, crypts and underground sites. [Dungeons and Taverns](https://modrinth.com/datapack/dungeons-and-taverns) broadens the range with temples, crypts, fortified places and larger dungeon complexes. Minecraft’s own structures remain part of exploration too.

These places offer different scales of expedition. Some are a short stop; others call for supplies and time to explore their rooms. Dungeon placement was reworked for the island world because complexes often hung without support. [How the island world works](../generation/) explains those changes.

## Wetlands

[Wetland Whimsy](https://modrinth.com/mod/wetland-whimsy) gives swamp journeys their own vegetation, creatures and connected ruins, from small remains to a dungeon and witch hut. Brushing suspicious mud reveals coins and other finds; pots and four kinds of incense connect excavation with ritual encounters. Bring a brush as well as supplies for exploring the ruins.

## Excavations and finds

[Better Archeology](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-archeology) adds excavation camps, buried remains, underwater sites and larger ruins. Brush suspicious blocks and fossil-bearing dirt to recover finds. Matching fossil heads and bodies can be assembled into displays, some with useful effects around the home.

Nine artifact shards make an unidentified artifact. An archeology table uses a separate brush to identify it, producing one of three artifact enchantments. Better brushes speed up this work. Other discoveries include totems, named weapons and maps, so an excavation can yield equipment as well as a collection piece.

## Inside the islands

[Galosphere](https://modrinth.com/mod/galosphere) adds crystal canyons, lichen caves and pink salt caves, along with forgotten ruins and a pink salt shrine. [Terralith](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith) and Minecraft contribute other cave environments and underground places. Looking inside an island can therefore reveal a different setting from the one visible on its surface.

## Marine habitats and fishing

[Spawn](https://modrinth.com/mod/spawn-mod) adds seagrass meadows, deep warm water and rocky shores, alongside small cold, sandy, tropical and volcanic islands, tide pools and the unique dodo island. These ecosystems share the existing water basins. Algal sand, reefstone, corals and different animals give them their own resources and activities. Inland ant gardens add wild ants and anthills: their gathering cycle changes nearby soil and wood into materials to excavate or collect.

[Hybrid Aquatic](https://modrinth.com/mod/hybrid-aquatic) brings marine animals, reefs, underwater plants and deeper habitats to the water basins inside large islands. Diving equipment supports longer visits below the surface, while underwater encounters and the bathyscaphe give expeditions further destinations and equipment to pursue. Marine habitats form irregular areas instead of the earlier repeating square grid. These habitats appear in newly generated terrain; previously explored water keeps its stored biomes and decoration.

[Tide](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tide) supplies the fishing system, including ordinary rods, special rods, bait and hook attachments. The fishing journal records catches and their conditions. In island basins, depth requirements follow the actual water beneath the line. Fit the Ominous Hook at the angling table for the aquatic encounter; it uses the same fishing system as ordinary catches.

To attach a hook, right-click an **Angling Table**. Put the rod in the top-left slot and the hook in the bottom-right slot, then take the finished rod from the bottom-left output. The other two right-hand slots accept fishing line at the top and a bobber in the middle. Right-clicking a rod with a hook or dropping one onto it in the inventory does not perform this exchange.

## Wildlife and companions

[Fowl Play](https://modrinth.com/mod/fowlplay) and [Hybrid Birds](https://modrinth.com/mod/hybrid-birds) add birds to forests, fields, wetlands and coasts. Their ducks, geese and gulls overlap, while other species broaden the range of habitats and calls. Ground and flight checks follow exposed island surfaces, including lower layers; empty space between islands does not become a spawning floor.

[Naturalist](https://modrinth.com/mod/naturalist) adds larger land animals and life around water, with useful companions among them. A tamed elephant can carry cargo, while a lion can help its owner in combat. Its birds remain part of the surrounding wildlife; head-perching is disabled in the pack.

[Critters and Companions](https://modrinth.com/mod/critters-and-companions) adds smaller animals with distinct uses. Combat insects, healing ladybugs, cargo-carrying roly-polies and digging ferrets support different activities. Silk supplies a grappling tool for working on exposed island faces. Aquatic companions help when diving, while the pearl necklace increases swimming speed and reduces the distance at which drowned detect you; its higher tiers also reduce detection by guardians.

## The Nether and End

These dimensions keep their familiar terrain and already contain additional [Dungeons and Taverns](https://modrinth.com/datapack/dungeons-and-taverns) structures. Nether journeys can lead to ports, keeps and piglin outposts. The End includes castles, lighthouses and ships alongside its familiar destinations.
