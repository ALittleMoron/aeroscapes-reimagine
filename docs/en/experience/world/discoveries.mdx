An expedition can lead to a new landscape, a village, a small ruined place or a large dungeon. Several mods contribute to this variety, with their places sharing the island terrain. This page describes their roles together; the [pack contents](../../../reference/mods/) page provides links to the individual projects.

## Landscapes and materials

Terralith and Nature’s Spirit give the islands much of their surface variety. Forests, flowered fields, dry regions, snowy landscapes and unusual rocky places make different islands recognizable. The selected Terralith landscapes include lavender valleys, sakura groves, deserts and oases, as well as volcanic and alpine regions.

Nature’s Spirit also adds trees and building materials, including maple, redwood and wisteria wood, chert and travertine. Bringing home a plant or a new material can be an expedition’s reward. These finds connect exploration with [gardens, cooking and building](../../building-and-daily-life/).

Custom Biome Saplings lets suitable saplings grow trees associated with their biome. It carries some of that regional character into growing and tending trees.

## Villages and everyday places

ChoiceTheorem’s Overhauled Village adds villages and outposts in different sizes and regional styles. Nature’s Spirit contributes cypress, wisteria, adobe and coconut villages. These are places to encounter while traveling; the planned player-developed colony system has its own role in [settlements](../../settlements/).

Dungeons and Taverns adds smaller scenes alongside its larger adventures: taverns, camps, wells, graveyards and remnants of earlier buildings. Terralith contributes ruins and small places such as huts, lodges and outposts. A modest discovery can give an island a landmark without filling it with a large dungeon.

## Ruins and larger adventures

Philips Ruins adds weathered remains, towers, crypts and underground sites. Dungeons and Taverns broadens the range with temples, crypts, fortified places and larger dungeon complexes. Minecraft’s own structures remain part of exploration too.

These places offer different scales of expedition. Some are a short stop; others call for supplies and time to explore their rooms. Dungeon placement was reworked for the island world because complexes often hung without support. [How the island world works](../generation/) explains those changes.

## Inside the islands

Galosphere adds crystal canyons, lichen caves and pink salt caves, along with forgotten ruins and a pink salt shrine. Terralith and Minecraft contribute other cave environments and underground places. Looking inside an island can therefore reveal a different setting from the one visible on its surface.

## Marine habitats and fishing

[Hybrid Aquatic](https://modrinth.com/mod/hybrid-aquatic) brings marine animals, reefs, underwater plants and deeper habitats to the water basins inside large islands. Diving equipment supports longer visits below the surface, while underwater encounters and the bathyscaphe give expeditions further destinations and equipment to pursue. Marine habitats form irregular areas instead of the earlier repeating square grid. These habitats appear in newly generated terrain; previously explored water keeps its stored biomes and decoration.

[Tide](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tide) supplies the fishing system, including ordinary rods, special rods, bait and hook attachments. The fishing journal records catches and their conditions. In island basins, depth requirements follow the actual water beneath the line. Fit the Ominous Hook at the angling table for the aquatic encounter; it uses the same fishing system as ordinary catches.

To attach a hook, right-click an **Angling Table**. Put the rod in the top-left slot and the hook in the bottom-right slot, then take the finished rod from the bottom-left output. The other two right-hand slots accept fishing line at the top and a bobber in the middle. Right-clicking a rod with a hook or dropping one onto it in the inventory does not perform this exchange.

## The Nether and End

These dimensions keep their familiar terrain and already contain additional Dungeons and Taverns structures. Nether journeys can lead to ports, keeps and piglin outposts. The End includes castles, lighthouses and ships alongside its familiar destinations.
