An expedition can lead to a new landscape, a village, an excavation or a large dungeon. Wildlife, wetland ruins and inhabited vessels give journeys smaller encounters along the way. This page describes how these discoveries fit together; the [pack contents](../../../reference/mods/) page lists the selected files.

## Landscapes and materials

[Terralith](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith) and [Nature’s Spirit](https://modrinth.com/mod/natures-spirit) give the islands much of their surface variety. Forests, flowered fields, dry regions, snowy landscapes and unusual rocky places make different islands recognizable. The selected [Terralith](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith) landscapes include lavender valleys, sakura groves, deserts and oases, as well as volcanic and alpine regions.

[Nature’s Spirit](https://modrinth.com/mod/natures-spirit) also adds trees and building materials, including maple, redwood and wisteria wood, chert and travertine. Bringing home a plant or a new material can be an expedition’s reward. These finds connect exploration with [gardens, cooking and building](../../building-and-daily-life/).

[Custom Biome Saplings](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith-biome-saplings) lets suitable saplings grow trees associated with their biome. It carries some of that regional character into growing and tending trees.

[This Rocks!](https://modrinth.com/mod/this-rocks-forge) adds loose stones, sticks, pinecones and coastal debris. These details can appear on exposed lower islands as well as the highest surface, with shells and starfish on basin floors. Collecting debris supplies small amounts of ordinary materials; stone fragments also have crafting recipes.

## Villages and everyday places

[ChoiceTheorem’s Overhauled Village](https://modrinth.com/mod/ct-overhaul-village) adds villages and outposts in different sizes and regional styles. [Nature’s Spirit](https://modrinth.com/mod/natures-spirit) contributes cypress, wisteria, adobe and coconut villages. These are places to encounter while traveling; the planned player-developed colony system has its own role in [settlements](../../settlements/).

[Dungeons and Taverns](https://modrinth.com/datapack/dungeons-and-taverns) adds smaller scenes alongside its larger adventures: taverns, camps, wells, graveyards and remnants of earlier buildings. [Terralith](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith) contributes ruins and small places such as huts, lodges and outposts. A modest discovery can give an island a landmark without filling it with a large dungeon.

[Sky Villages](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sky-villages-forge) adds settlements high in the sky. They appear farther apart in newly explored terrain. Their houses, platforms and bridges form a complete village below the world's upper boundary. Reaching the settlement and finding room to land become part of an aerial expedition.

## Island stops

On ordinary flights, watch for woodpiles, carts, wells and low shelters in clearings or on walkable terraces. Timber, sandstone and bamboo distinguish finds in different landscapes. Larger finds, such as covered wagons and the occasional windmill, are spaced farther apart. Look for the way onto the site from the island, especially on steep terrain.

New surface discoveries appear in newly generated chunks. With commands enabled, try `/locate structure mvs:cart` or `/locate structure formationsoverworld:wagon`. The same sites can be found with Explorer’s Compass.

Look for Moog’s desert houses on walkable, dry ground in sandy biomes and for Formations Overworld igloos in snow-covered terrain. Their search IDs are `mvs:desert_house` and `formationsoverworld:igloo`.

Look for a striped canopy and a column of campfire smoke. A survey camp's barrel holds a small ration and field supplies. Surviving gardens grow wheat and carrots on patches of level ground. A low entrance in a mossy bank leads into a fox den.

Some camps in newly explored terrain have a sign at the edge of the canopy. Read it from the camp side, then follow its distances and turns from the sign to an exposed ore face. Bring a pickaxe suited to the ore named on the sign.

Look for widely spaced wooden landing decks on open island edges. Approach from the sky and step ashore between the lanterns. Yellow markings show the clear part of the deck. Some stops have a small shelter, workshop, supply cache or another scene just inland. Water stops occur on suitable wet islands. These stops appear in newly generated terrain.

Use Explorer’s Compass to search for an individual stop. With commands enabled, run `/locate structure` followed by its ID.

## Vessels in the sky

[Creat Aeronautics Structures](https://modrinth.com/mod/creat-aeronautics-structures/version/aMCksPon) supplies four intact aerial encounters: merchant and pillager balloons and dirigibles. Merchant decks offer trading stops; pillager crews make the other pair hostile destinations with loot to search. These vessels are static structures. Plan the approach to their decks and the return journey as you would for another island destination.

Abandoned cargo airships have a timber hull and an idle drive. The ivory and orange observation balloons carry a map station and a few supplies. At one airship, a winch on the ledge still holds a rope to the bow. A lantern marks the approach. Use an iron grappling hook on the timber beside the bow ladder and climb to the deck, or pull alongside the crossbeam to board from your ship. Both routes lead to the same cargo barrel. These sites appear in new chunks and can be searched with Explorer’s Compass or `/locate structure`.

Look for twin envelopes over cargo barges, narrow cliff skiffs, and salvage tugs with a working deck behind the wheelhouse. Other abandoned finds include survey catamarans, mail cutters, passenger ferries, weather ships and glasshouse carriers. Find the boarding ladder or ramp before committing to an approach.

## Ruins and larger adventures

[Explorify](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/explorify) adds campsites, farmsteads, watchtowers, supply caches and shrines. These give shorter journeys places to stop alongside the larger dungeons. Their foundations and connecting paths must fit the island terrain.

[Philips Ruins](https://modrinth.com/mod/philips-ruins) adds weathered remains, towers, crypts and underground sites. [Dungeons and Taverns](https://modrinth.com/datapack/dungeons-and-taverns) broadens the range with temples, crypts, fortified places and larger dungeon complexes. Minecraft’s own structures remain part of exploration too.

These places offer different scales of expedition. Some are a short stop; others call for supplies and time to explore their rooms. Dungeon placement was reworked for the island world because complexes often hung without support. [How the island world works](../generation/) explains those changes.

## Wetlands

[Ribbits](https://modrinth.com/mod/ribbits) adds small swamp villages with musicians, gardeners, fishers and merchants. Listen for a group performance or buy a maraca from a merchant for six to eight amethyst shards and play along.

[Wetland Whimsy](https://modrinth.com/mod/wetland-whimsy) gives swamp journeys their own vegetation, creatures and connected ruins, from small remains to a dungeon and witch hut. Brushing suspicious mud reveals coins and other finds; pots and four kinds of incense connect excavation with ritual encounters. Bring a brush as well as supplies for exploring the ruins.

## Excavations and finds

[Better Archeology](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-archeology) adds excavation camps, buried remains, underwater sites and larger ruins. Brush suspicious blocks and fossil-bearing dirt to recover finds. Matching fossil heads and bodies can be assembled into displays, some with useful effects around the home.

Nine artifact shards make an unidentified artifact. An archeology table uses a separate brush to identify it, producing one of three artifact enchantments. Better brushes speed up this work. Other discoveries include totems, named weapons and maps, so an excavation can yield equipment as well as a collection piece.

## Inside the islands

[Galosphere](https://modrinth.com/mod/galosphere) adds crystal canyons, lichen caves and pink salt caves, along with forgotten ruins and a pink salt shrine. [Terralith](https://modrinth.com/datapack/terralith) and Minecraft contribute other cave environments and underground places. Looking inside an island can therefore reveal a different setting from the one visible on its surface.

Strike an exposed amethyst, allurite or lumiere cluster to hear nearby clusters in the same open pocket ring in response.

## Marine habitats and fishing

[Spawn](https://modrinth.com/mod/spawn-mod) adds seagrass meadows, deep warm water and rocky shores, alongside small cold, sandy, tropical and volcanic islands, tide pools and the unique dodo island. These ecosystems share the existing water basins. Algal sand, reefstone, corals and different animals give them their own resources and activities. Inland ant gardens add wild ants and anthills: their gathering cycle changes nearby soil and wood into materials to excavate or collect.

[Hybrid Aquatic](https://modrinth.com/mod/hybrid-aquatic) brings marine animals, reefs, underwater plants and deeper habitats to the water basins inside large islands. Diving equipment supports longer visits below the surface, while underwater encounters and the bathyscaphe give expeditions further destinations and equipment to pursue. Marine habitats form irregular areas instead of the earlier repeating square grid. These habitats appear in newly generated terrain; previously explored water keeps its stored biomes and decoration.

[Tide](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tide) supplies the fishing system, including ordinary rods, special rods, bait and hook attachments. The fishing journal records catches and their conditions. In island basins, depth requirements follow the actual water beneath the line. Fit the Ominous Hook at the angling table for the aquatic encounter; it uses the same fishing system as ordinary catches.

To attach a hook, right-click an **Angling Table**. Put the rod in the top-left slot and the hook in the bottom-right slot, then take the finished rod from the bottom-left output. The other two right-hand slots accept fishing line at the top and a bobber in the middle. Right-clicking a rod with a hook or dropping one onto it in the inventory does not perform this exchange.

## Predators and prey

Naturalist bears and Buzzier Bees grizzlies hunt turkeys. Alligators prey on ducks and geese, lions on ostriches, tigers on peacocks, and snakes on rats. Naturalist's great white sharks can hunt herring, bluefish and mackerel.

## Cliff ledges and roosts

Fly alongside exposed island ledges to see gull colonies. At dusk, pass below overhangs to catch bats leaving their roosts. Groups of hanging roots and long vines grow beneath overhangs. Most are dark, with occasional patches of dim light. These sites form in new terrain.

A close explosion or lightning strike can scatter gulls and startle deer. Crouch a few blocks from a wild deer and let it watch you for a while. Once it grows accustomed to you, it will allow a slow approach. A sudden rush can still send it running.

## Sky rays and cloud jellies

In the open air between Overworld islands, a sky ray may swim past your ship. Watch the stroke travel from the base of its broad fins to their flexible tips. Between strokes it can glide with its fins spread and bank into a turn. You can fly alongside it safely. If you strike it, watch its wings for the counterattack and give it room to break away.

Occasionally a young ray with shorter fins swims beside an adult. Watch for the smaller silhouette banking after its companion.

Cloud jellies drift together, each swimming to its own rhythm. As a jelly turns onto its side, the crown points along its next stroke. The bell squeezes, reopens and stays open while the animal glides. Its longest tentacles trail round the turn. Most are small, but a colony can include a much larger individual. Giants the size of a whale are exceptionally rare.

A fringed dome trails fine strands around its rim. A sea nettle also has folded oral arms in the middle. The copper mane has a broad bell with tufts beneath it, while the cloud lantern has a tall bell and hanging pairs of ribbons. Each form can be small or large, and an individual keeps its form and bell size when the world reloads. The longest strands reach well below the rest. Touching the bell or a strand causes a sting and a brief amber flash. Give them room when gliding or standing on an open deck. A solid, enclosed cabin shelters its occupants during a crossing. Your first sting shows a short reminder about taking cover.

A soft glow pulses around each cloud jelly’s bell and makes it easier to spot at night.

## Sky whales

[Sky Whales](https://modrinth.com/mod/sky-whales) adds rare large creatures in the open Overworld sky above islands and gaps. They remain peaceful until attacked; provoking one exposes the hunter to a sonic attack. A slain whale leaves a physical carcass that can be harvested for meat and bones. Milling or crushing bones produces dust for Aeronautics' existing lift-material routes. The carcass remains for a limited time, so recovering and processing its cargo becomes part of the expedition.

The pack gives these island-like animals thirteen ecological forms: stone, mossy, rooted, fungal, granite, chalk, dripstone, basalt, lava, sandstone, reef, snowy and cloud. Their bodies and attached habitats have different shapes: ore-bearing rock, trees rooted on the back with small hollow logs, tall mushrooms, open geodes, weathered chalk arches, coral colonies and ice arches. Every form also reads as a whale from below: a lighter belly and jaw with throat grooves, broad pectoral fins and tail flukes, and patches of related stone along the flanks. The surfaces use the textures of natural world blocks from the installed game. Tail strokes, delayed fluke movement, fin adjustments, gliding and occasional blinks give the creatures movement even while hovering. Lava whales have basalt and magma crust with exposed lava in their craters and channels, and appear only above a source-lava lake. Cloud forms occupy the highest sky; snowy forms appear in the high open sky and above cold regions. A whale keeps its form when it moves or the world reloads.

Encounters favour clear space ahead of the traveller, including gaps between island layers. Over open void, the island you stand on decides the whale's form. Whale calls carry about 96 blocks. The whole body must fit already loaded chunks. Whales appear farther away and no longer disappear immediately when they cross a nearby distance boundary. Ordinary chunk unloading saves them; a whale that keeps ticking far from every player leaves after a grace period. Returning nearby resets that period.

Whales are not spread evenly across the sky. About a quarter of regions roughly a thousand blocks wide are whale waters, where encounters are twice as frequent; elsewhere they are rarer, so the overall number stays the same. Occasionally a whale travels in a pod with one or two companions of the same form, and a straggler swims back to its leader. When a calm leader calls, its nearby companions may answer in turn. A whale that is not fighting may accelerate to swim alongside a moving ship for half a minute to a minute. Watch its tail as it closes the gap and settles beside the hull. A whale can keep pace with a ship travelling between 2 and 10 blocks per second. A faster ship can pull away. Many whales are circled by small flocks of swifts. Photographs of whales count toward the [sky photo journal](../../building-and-daily-life/).

Treat a carcass as a temporary harvesting site. Its automatic cleanup removes the whole physical body, including any machinery or storage you attach; keep permanent builds and cargo on a separate ship.

### Whale falls

Islands sometimes hold the skeletons of whales that died long ago: a spine carried on rib arches, a skull with jaw bones, flippers and a tail, half sunk into the ground and touched by moss. Other skeletons have grown into island undersides and are seen from below. They are rare landmarks rather than stores of lift material: almost all bones are ordinary, with only a few cracked blocks and one or two intact whale bone blocks suitable for lift-material dust.

### See every form without searching

With commands enabled, fly into open Overworld sky and run `/aeroscapes_whales show stone`. A protected preview appears ahead and stays in place while its swimming animation plays. Use `/aeroscapes_whales next` to cycle through all thirteen forms, or replace `stone` with `moss`, `rooted`, `fungal`, `granite`, `chalk`, `dripstone`, `basalt`, `lava`, `sandstone`, `reef`, `snow` or `cloud`. Tab completes the names. Switching keeps the preview in the same place. Previews also make the native whale calls, with enough range to hear them from the initial viewing position. Calls occur intermittently; the Friendly Creatures volume slider controls them.

Use `/aeroscapes_whales clear` to remove your preview without leaving a carcass. These commands require operator permission and affect only your preview. If its chunk unloads, return to the coordinates shown in the message before switching or removing it. For an obstructed placement, move farther from islands and below the world's upper boundary.

## Wildlife and companions

[Fowl Play](https://modrinth.com/mod/fowlplay) and [Hybrid Birds](https://modrinth.com/mod/hybrid-birds) add birds to forests, fields, wetlands and coasts. Their ducks, geese and gulls overlap, while other species broaden the range of habitats and calls. Ground and flight checks follow exposed island surfaces, including lower layers; empty space between islands does not become a spawning floor.

[Naturalist](https://modrinth.com/mod/naturalist) adds larger land animals and life around water, with useful companions among them. A tamed elephant can carry cargo, while a lion can help its owner in combat. Its birds remain part of the surrounding wildlife; head-perching is disabled in the pack.

[Critters and Companions](https://modrinth.com/mod/critters-and-companions) adds smaller animals with distinct uses. Combat insects, healing ladybugs, cargo-carrying roly-polies and digging ferrets support different activities. Aquatic companions help when diving, while the pearl necklace increases swimming speed and reduces the distance at which drowned detect you; its higher tiers also reduce detection by guardians.

## The Nether and End

These dimensions keep their familiar terrain and already contain additional [Dungeons and Taverns](https://modrinth.com/datapack/dungeons-and-taverns) structures. Nether journeys can lead to ports, keeps and piglin outposts. The End includes castles, lighthouses and ships alongside its familiar destinations.
