The pack already includes additional character animations, detailed skin layers and equipment enchantments. Races, classes, character progression and the intended action-combat system are planned additions.

## Enchantments and character rendering

[Allurement](https://modrinth.com/project/eIO12l2t) adds enchantments and changes parts of enchanting, including the ability to enchant horse armor.

Fresh Animations: Player Extension, Not Enough Animations and 3D Skin Layers change how the character is shown. Their roles are described under [graphics, sound and animation](../quality-of-life/presentation/). [Convenience](../quality-of-life/) covers inventory recovery through Corpse.

## Races and classes

Races should differ through appearance, abilities, size, health and interactions with the world. Humans, goblins and dwarves suit the setting; the complete roster remains to be selected. Advantages and disadvantages should be understandable before selection. A race with innate flight may have that ability from the beginning, even when it makes island travel much easier.

Classes and specializations should support mixed builds. A warrior who learns [magic](../magic/) is a valid character. Specialists can excel while other skills remain useful. The preferred direction is development through use, with peaceful and combat activities improving related abilities; the actual progression system has not been selected.

Character choices should last. A rare, expensive change may become possible, but its method and treatment of existing progress remain undecided.

## Combat and camera

Otherworld D&D is the reference for expressive animation, meaningful weapon differences and readable hits. Melee, ranged weapons and magic should remain viable styles. The intended equipment can include firearms suited to the setting, ship cannons and explosives alongside swords, bows and crossbows.

Both first and third person should support the main activities. The side-offset third-person camera is already supplied by Shoulder Surfing, independently of the planned combat changes; [graphics and animation](../quality-of-life/presentation/#character-and-camera) describes its controls.

Danger should depend mainly on the place: its creatures, biome, dungeon, height and dimension. Moderate growth of threats over time is also desired; the rules remain to be chosen.
