The pack already includes additional character animations, detailed skin layers and equipment enchantments. Races, classes, character progression and the intended action-combat system are planned additions.

## Enchantments and character rendering

[Allurement](https://modrinth.com/project/eIO12l2t) adds enchantments and changes parts of enchanting, including the ability to enchant horse armor.

[Fresh Animations: Player Extension](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/fa-player-extension), [Not Enough Animations](https://modrinth.com/project/MPCX6s5C) and [3D Skin Layers](https://modrinth.com/mod/3dskinlayers) change how the character is shown. Their roles are described under [graphics, sound and animation](../quality-of-life/presentation/). [Convenience](../quality-of-life/) covers inventory recovery through [Corpse](https://modrinth.com/mod/corpse).

## Races and classes

Races should differ through appearance, abilities, size, health and interactions with the world. Humans, dwarves, goblins and elves are selected for the first roster before `1.0.0`; that roster may be refined as the pack develops. Advantages and disadvantages should be understandable before selection. Races that grant unrestricted flight from the start will be excluded or adapted so their movement abilities preserve the challenge of crossing islands. Other meaningful advantages and disadvantages remain welcome.

Classes and specializations should support mixed builds. A warrior who learns [magic](../magic/) is a valid character. Specialists can excel while other skills remain useful. The preferred direction is development through use, with peaceful and combat activities improving related abilities; the actual progression system has not been selected.

The initial classes are warrior, ranger, mage and rogue; profession is a separate choice. The initial professions are blacksmith, builder, farmer and cook.

Subclasses are planned later, before `1.0.0`, offering more distinct specializations after the basic character system. Their exact roster remains to be defined. Characters will be able to learn several specializations of their class through skill mastery, without mandatory trials or NPC training. Learned specializations should work together, combining passive benefits with a few active techniques. Any limits on talent-point allocation remain to be determined.

Race, class and profession switching is not planned through `1.0.0`. Characters can learn other skills and combine directions without resetting those choices.

## Combat and camera

[Otherworld D&D](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/otherworld-dnd) is the reference for expressive animation, meaningful weapon differences and readable hits. Melee, ranged weapons and magic should remain viable styles. The intended equipment can include firearms suited to the setting, ship cannons and explosives alongside swords, bows and crossbows.

Both first and third person should support the main activities. The side-offset third-person camera is already supplied by [Shoulder Surfing](https://modrinth.com/mod/shoulder-surfing-reloaded), independently of the planned combat changes; [graphics and animation](../quality-of-life/presentation/#character-and-camera) describes its controls.

Danger should depend mainly on the place: its creatures, biome, dungeon, height and dimension. Moderate growth of threats over time is also desired; the rules remain to be chosen.
