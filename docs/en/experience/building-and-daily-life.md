Building blocks, furniture, decorative details and kitchen equipment help furnish a home, workshop or cabin. Farming and cooking offer activities around a home and supplies for journeys.

## Building and decoration

[Quark](https://modrinth.com/project/qnQsVE2z) and [Supplementaries](https://modrinth.com/project/fFEIiSDQ) add building and decorative blocks alongside practical household objects. Nature’s Spirit connects some of the material variety with the biomes players explore.

[Handcrafted](https://modrinth.com/mod/handcrafted) supplies a coordinated set of beds, nightstands, kitchen counters, cupboards and smaller household decorations. [Chipped](https://modrinth.com/mod/chipped) expands the surface patterns of familiar building materials. Its workbenches and handheld tools open a conversion menu for choosing a supported block variant; they serve a different purpose from furniture.

[wind chimes](https://modrinth.com/mod/windchime) adds bamboo, copper and iron chimes for homes and ship interiors. They sway and ring in the wind, react to interaction and redstone, and respond to acceleration and turns aboard Sable ships. Their recipes use ordinary sticks, string, buttons and the corresponding material.

Some ordinary furniture recipes share ingredients with existing blocks. [Polymorph’s result selector](../quality-of-life/#recipes-and-information) lets players choose between matching recipes in supported crafting menus. Create mechanical crafters use their own recipe lookup.

Diagonal Fences, Diagonal Walls and Diagonal Windows extend the ways fences, walls and windows connect. Dramatic Doors adds tall and short doors; Reconnectible Chains connects fences with decorative chains. Together, these additions offer ways to shape an island house, workshop, town or ship interior.

Conversions to tall Chipped doors are available where the corresponding ordinary door exists in the included Chipped version.

Create’s schematics and building tools support larger projects. [Engineering](../engineering/) describes how construction connects with moving machinery, while [convenience](../quality-of-life/) covers rearranging supported blocks and organizing supplies.

## Food and household activities

Farmer’s Delight and its additions expand ingredients and cooking. Create’s food additions connect those activities with mechanical production. A garden can supply a kitchen, and a production line can prepare stores for a journey. [Cooking and farming](food/) explains how these groups fit together.

Comforts adds portable sleeping bags and hammocks without changing the player’s spawn point. Comfortable Campfires gives nearby players Farmer’s Delight’s Comfort effect while the campfire is lit.

## Photography and music

[Exposure](https://modrinth.com/mod/exposure) adds film photography for recording journeys and decorating a home. Load film into a camera, take photographs through its viewfinder, then develop the film and print photographs at a lightroom. The resulting photographs can be displayed in the world.

[Immersive Melodies](https://modrinth.com/mod/immersive-melodies) adds musical instruments with a choice of melodies and manual playing. They provide a household or camp activity alongside the existing background ambience. By default, playing an instrument interrupts the game's background music.

## Planned survival systems

Nutrition groups, thirst with water quality, and moderate heat and cold are planned additions. They should make varied meals, drinking water, equipment and shelter useful without constant interruption. Ordinary hunger will remain. [Cooking and farming](food/#planned-nutrition-and-thirst) describes the intended food and water mechanics.

Seasons remain optional. Their interaction with food supplies and drinking water is part of [household planning](food/#seasons-and-household-planning).
