## 0.7.5

- Some survey camps have a field sign that leads along a short walk to exposed ore on the same island.

## 0.7.4

- Find abandoned airships and observation balloons with Create and Aeronautics fittings in newly explored skies. A working mooring line and a broken tether mark the ledge approach to one airship. The ladder and the ship approach lead to the same cargo.
- Survey campfires send up signal smoke. Lost cargo balloons drift down and can be collected by hand or shot down.
- Rare study scrolls from challenging expeditions teach Deep Breath or Distraction Throw. Use a scroll once per character and trade any spare copies.
- Both study scrolls have new icons and rolled-paper models in hand. The Ring of Invisibility has a visible icon and hand texture.
- Exceptional rings lend Invisibility I or Frostwave I, and an exceptional Pearl Necklace III stores air to share. Hand crafting can produce the exceptional Frostward Ring or necklace, with better odds at higher Crafting levels.

## 0.7.3

- New island terrain can hold widely spaced survey camps with basic supplies, gardens with connected stone walls on level ground, and mossy fox burrows. Lantern-marked landing decks sit apart on open island edges with clear approaches and connected railings. Each type is searchable with Explorer’s Compass or `/locate structure`.
- Ribbits villages bring music and trading to suitable island swamps. Their buildings use a compact layout and short supports.

## 0.7.2

- Gull colonies gather on exposed island ledges. Bats fly out from sheltered roosts at dusk.
- Clusters of hanging roots and long vines grow beneath island overhangs. A few carry a patch of dim light.
- Nearby booms and lightning can startle gulls and deer. Wild deer can grow accustomed to a quiet observer.
- Whale pods sometimes answer their leader's call one at a time.
- A young sky ray can sometimes be seen swimming beside an adult.
- Players and animals leave short-lived footprints on solid surfaces.
- Nearby crystal clusters answer when you strike one in the same open pocket.

## 0.7.1

- Small floating islands use a shared surface biome across their connected terrain, including thin edges and islands stacked at different heights.
- Combat, Magic and Professions now earn and spend talent points independently. Existing learned talents move to their own trees.
- Potions no longer yield skill XP. Rewards for combat, personal crafting, repairs, enchanting, harvests, catches and breeding now reflect the action performed.
- Typing the glossary key in inventory search leaves the search open. Use Ctrl with the glossary key to inspect a hovered item.
- Reduced MakeUp distance fog and lightened deep shadows under islands.

## 0.7.0

- The character and progression stage brings together races, starting classes and professions, practice-based skills, talents and the shared ability wheel.
- Pick an ear or beard color from a palette with a live preview during character creation or from the character sheet. Resetting restores the color from your skin for ears or the original brown for beards.
- Equipped rings, necklaces and supported belt accessories are visible when their Curios slot is set to show them.
- Practice XP appears below Jade's target information. Item tooltips no longer show Project MMO's estimated XP awards.
- Talents available to learn have a bright green frame, and learned talents have a checkmark on a dark badge.
- Smooth Scrolling animates chat, lists, creative inventory and hotbar scrolling. Tiny Item Animations animates items as you pick them up or put them down with the cursor.
- Animated Inventory shows items moving between slots, including stacks transferred with Shift-click.
- Consecutive attacks with daggers and kitchen knives cycle through a forward jab, a slicing cut and an overhead reverse-grip stab in both camera views. The moves mirror for left-handed players.
- Enable Large starting island on the World tab for a mountainous start with several biomes and nearby woodland. The option is off by default.
- New galleons need clear water and room for the hull and sails. Forgotten ruins need support beneath their platforms.
- Weeds spread into empty spaces on farmland. Water bottles revive wilted saplings with their original growth stage. Fall deposits leave plants and decorations in place, and clearing them reveals the grass underneath.
- Immersive Weathering spreads moss to selected Nature's Spirit and Quark masonry, cracks travertine and chert, and covers Create's cut scoria bricks with snow. Galosphere lichen spreads moss, flowing water leaves red sand below pink sandstone, and lit stoves and lemonstone braziers leave soot.
- Bears can hunt turkeys. Alligators, lions, tigers, snakes and great white sharks can hunt additional prey from the pack's fauna.
- A soft, pulsing halo surrounds the bells of cloud jellies, and the jelly bodies retain shading from the scene.

## 0.6.8

- Draw spells with Hex Casting staves using amethyst from geodes. Assign a programmed Cypher, Trinket or Artifact to the ability wheel and cast from its own charge.
- Reforged staves help locate a failed pattern. Reforged program carriers report charge and failures. Reforging a jeweler's hammer can save durability and collect drops.
- Characters carry full-length Hex Casting staves near the middle of the curved shaft. Staff heads and bindings vary by material. The jeweler's hammer handle sits across the palm, and Hex books use the same grip as the Chipped Alchemy Book.
- Staff tips stay in view in first person. Scrolls have a partly unrolled edge and the scrying lens has a broad glass bevel. Program carriers have distinct settings, and faceted media phials show their fill level and color.
- The `make model-preview` command renders an offline gallery of held items, sky rays and all four cloud jelly forms.
- Hex movement patterns share a recovery time per target. Blink, Greater Teleport, impulse, levitation, adjustable effect levels and explosions have limits.

## 0.6.7

- Sky rays propel themselves with broad fin strokes, glide through banked turns and defend themselves when attacked.
- Cloud jellies drift in mixed-size colonies with four distinct forms, from a fringed dome to a tall lantern. They turn their bells into a stroke and glide on their sides with their tentacles trailing. Their bells and tentacles sting on contact, and enclosed ship cabins shelter their occupants.
- Sky whales adjust their tail strokes as they accelerate to swim alongside a moving ship.
- Local and CI builds now compile the mod against the same Minecraft classes so they produce identical JARs.

## 0.6.6

- An Identity Seal from exploration chests changes your race, main class or profession. Preview the change and choose whether to refund your old class branch.
- Craft Knotted Thread to redistribute learned talents. The tree shows affected talents and the 10% penalty before confirmation. New practice can restore lost points.

## 0.6.5

- Choose a profession and one of two peaceful starting talents alongside your race and class. Existing characters complete their missing choices and receive their profession kit once.
- Eight learnable profession talents share ten points with gathering. Manually prepared meals can carry their cook's benefit to another player.

## 0.6.4

- Choose a class after race and appearance in the same window. Review all choices, advantages, drawbacks and combined practice bonuses before confirming or reopen the sheet from the inventory portrait.
- Warriors, rangers and rogues choose a starting combat talent. Mages start with eight rank-1 spells and a casting focus. Six physical talents are open to all classes, bringing the shared combat budget to 142 points.

## 0.6.3

- Choose a human, dwarf, goblin or elf before play, with three appearances per race on a stationary preview of your own skin. The portrait button in the inventory reopens your confirmed character sheet.
- Singleplayer pauses during character creation. In multiplayer, the character is protected from damage until the choice is confirmed.
- Races have distinct dimensions, health and stamina. Dwarves resist cold and knockback, goblins fit through one-block hatches when crouched, and elves move and learn archery and magic faster.
- Prepare dwarf night vision or a temporary following light for elves in the same eight slots as spells.
- Mark low and full-height passages with Chalk. Four freely available loom motifs can decorate banners and shields.
- A worn Amulet of Teleportation grants a preparable 16-block step with a 20-second cooldown. Corrected which necklace drops when its spell power requirement is unmet.
- Accessories with special effects have item descriptions and EMI information pages. Right-click equips an accessory from your hand. For items with a use of their own, sneak and right-click in the air to equip them.
- The character stays visible next to climbing ropes and while climbing them. Enlarging the minimap no longer shares the spyglass zoom key.
- Fixed a world crash when a dropped sapling plants itself. Manual placement still grants building practice.

- Corrected the next-point display when weapon and magic practice together fill the combat budget.
- Fixed an Animal Husbandry cow-loot crash and cooked direct beef and golden beef rewards on burning or lethal magical-fire deaths.
- Interrupted scroll channels consume one scroll if they have already produced an effect.
- The preparation catalogue defaults to learned spells, with a toggle for the full list, explicit unavailable states and assignment-rejection messages.
- Reduced the prepared-spell HUD to an icon and name at the bottom left; the empty-slot message disappears after five seconds.
- Fixed overlapping talent frames and inaccurate selection when zooming out; centering now fits the complete tree.
- Fire spells now trigger cooked death drops even on a lethal first hit. Added missing cooking to 34 creature loot tables and repaired fire-source and enchantment conditions for ash and tallow.
- Rebuilt magic as one 131-node tree with four functional directions: eight starter spells, 33 later bundled spells and 70 individual unlocks cover all 111 spells, with useful improvements between mastery gates.
- Added an independent magic practice curve and staged point rewards up to the shared 136-point combat cap; magic practice alone can fund the complete tree and continues earning points after level 50.
- Magic XP now follows actual discounted mana paid per casting pulse, original mana cost for scroll pulses and one XP for a free recast, without repetition fatigue or source caps.
- Added eight shared preparation slots with manual assignment, clearing and rearrangement from the inventory and Curios, a selection wheel and optional unbound cast, wheel and slot-selection keys.
- Learned spells start at rank 1; inscription-table study consumes one higher-rank scroll only when it improves personal knowledge within the direction's mastery limit. Too-high permanent study remains rejected intact, without downranking or stored future ranks.
- Consumable scrolls need no personal talent, mastery, rank, knowledge, discovery, focus or preparation; their native source, targeting and travel restrictions remain, and they do not teach permanent knowledge.
- Refunded former magic-tree allocations once while preserving physical and profession talents, saved personal spell ranks and preparation; permanent use requires relearning the new unlocks.
- Enabled Recall scrolls and fixed one-scroll consumption when a successful dimension-changing cast clears its native casting state.
- Spell books are required equipped focuses that keep their bonuses while personal preparation replaces book capacity. Knowledge and slots persist through death, relogging and dimension changes; earlier book inscriptions do not grant personal knowledge or talent points.
- Kept native Eldritch Manuscript discovery requirements alongside the seven Eldritch spell nodes.
- Enabled Angel Wings as temporary elytra-style flight, capped at 50 seconds after bonuses with at least 120 seconds between activations.
- Enabled temporary Portal pairs within one dimension and 128 blocks, lasting 20, 40 or 60 seconds by rank with a 60-second cap and at least 180 seconds between completed pairs; disabled the Portal spell's permanent frame links.
- Included the shader settings and combat talent data required by quality checks in the owned mod's source archive so it can build outside the repository.
