## Where to post

Post ideas in GitHub Issues so suggestions can be found and discussed in one place. Search for similar ideas first. If there's already a discussion, add your details in a comment. For a new idea, open a separate issue with a short, descriptive title. [Open GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ALittleMoron/aeroscapes-reimagine/issues)

Describe the situation in which the idea would apply, what you'd change, and how it would help or interest players. If you suggest a mod or add-on, link to it and name the feature the pack is missing. If you noticed something while playing, include your pack version.

## What kinds of ideas fit

Create is the foundation for engineering, and Aeronautics is the foundation for flight. Add-ons that extend them in specific ways are welcome suggestions.

- **Adventures and discoveries.** New places, mobs, and encounters players can find while exploring the islands or spot from an airship.
- **World generation.** Changes of limited scope are welcome, such as a new biome, natural landmark, or structure that fits the island terrain.
- **Quality of life (QoL).** Mods and changes that make everyday tasks easier where the pack's existing features leave a gap.
- **Visuals and atmosphere.** Particles, visual effects, and mods that bring the world to life and fit its style.
- **Integrations.** Missing interactions between mods or with vanilla Minecraft mechanics. For example, an idea might use Immersive Weathering to give particular blocks mossy, rusty, snow-covered, or sand-covered variants. Another idea might have a specific physically demanding action draw on the pack's existing stamina.

## What not to suggest

- **Large, content-heavy mods.** The main mods for engineering, settlements, magic, combat, and other core systems have already been selected, and the roadmap sets out how they will develop. Adding a mod of comparable scope would require substantial rebalancing, integration with other mods, and ongoing maintenance. The additional load could lower the server's tick rate (TPS).
- **Redundant mechanics and recipes.** When several systems serve the same purpose or several recipes lead to the same items without a clear benefit, it becomes harder for players to tell which option to use and what to learn next.
- **A full replacement for island generation.** World-generation ideas should build on the existing island world. Switching to a different world type is outside the project's scope.
- **Requests to add rivers.** After five unsuccessful attempts, the author took rivers off the roadmap and ended their own work on river generation. Please do not open an issue requesting rivers. If you've implemented working river generation for the island world yourself, submit a pull request for review. A seed, coordinates, and in-game screenshots would help show how the river channel and banks fit the terrain.

## How suggestions are considered

The author considers how the idea would benefit players and how it fits the world and the chosen systems. Compatibility with the pack and ongoing maintenance also matter when considering new mods. The author decides which ideas to include and when.
