[Isabel’s Aeroscapes](https://modrinth.com/modpack/aeroscapes), by Isabel / nuclearobelisk, is the reference for island survival, Create engineering and Aeronautics travel. Reimagine adapts selected data from its published 1.0.8 release and develops a separate modpack around that foundation.

[Otherworld D&D](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/otherworld-dnd) inspires the planned character, magic and combat experience. Its code and assets have not been copied into the pack.

The engineering foundation comes from [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create) and [Create Aeronautics](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-aeronautics). Isabel’s [Aero Islands](https://modrinth.com/project/tGVwBATM) inspired the owned island generator. The owned mod incorporates selected Aero worldgen data and retains GPL-3.0-or-later for 0.1.0, with their origin and modifications recorded in its notices. Terralith, Nature’s Spirit, Galosphere and the included structure projects complete the world. Food, decoration, interface, sound and performance contributions make everyday play more convenient alongside these larger systems.

The [pack contents](../reference/mods/) page links to the exact third-party releases and their authors. Individual projects retain their own terms; adapting a reference pack does not replace those terms. Reimagine is independent of the reference projects and does not imply their authors’ endorsement.

The owned mod’s complete corresponding source and build instructions accompany the pack in `licenses/aeroscapes-reimagine-source.zip`; `SOURCE-MANIFEST.json` identifies the matching binary. Original third-party JARs retain their licenses. Nature’s Spirit data adaptations retain Team Hibiscus’s MIT copyright and license notice in `licenses/Natures-Spirit-data-MIT.txt`.
