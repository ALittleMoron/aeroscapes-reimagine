Aeroscapes Reimagine adds conditions to the sixteen original Dye Depot catalyst
self-drop tables so NeoForge reads them only when Dye Depot and the target item
are present. The editable JSON is shipped in the required compatibility data
pack under data/create_connected/loot_table/blocks/. Create: Connected's
Russian names for the affected components are reused in its configuration
labels. New Russian text fills the pinned provider's missing gameplay strings
and localizes configuration labels, comments and units. Localization source
and the upstream notice accompany the Aeroscapes Reimagine mod source archive.

Aeroscapes Reimagine adds item existence conditions to the two original
self-drop tables for orange_sensitiveglass and industrial_blinking_led, whose
items are absent in the pinned pack. Editable JSON ships in the required
compatibility data pack under data/rsgauges/loot_table/blocks/. The owned
mod supplies adjusted English and Russian item help, configuration text and
an independently authored palette for the status overlay.
