Licensing declarations
The port publisher's CurseForge project declares LGPL version 2.1. The
distributed mod descriptor and source gradle.properties declare MIT, as do
the original author's official CurseForge and Modrinth projects. The inherited
source LICENSE.txt is a Forge/FML notice and expressly distinguishes ordinary
mods from Forge's own LGPL scope. It is not treated as a new relicensing notice.

This distribution preserves both declarations and follows the obligations of
both: the original JAR remains unchanged and replaceable, full corresponding
source accompanies it, upstream notices remain intact, and the MIT and LGPL-2.1
texts are supplied in LICENSES/. No assertion is made that the publisher
corrected its metadata or that one declaration overrides the other. No extra
restriction on modification or reverse engineering for debugging is added.

Source coverage
A clean JDK 21 rebuild using the unmodified Gradle wrapper and build scripts
produced all 221 classes byte-for-byte identical to the official JAR, with no
missing or additional class. The archive as a whole differs because of the
build-time manifest, line endings/JSON formatting and generated resources.
The complete upstream source tree, build scripts and wrapper are included.
Ten additional plain JSON resources present only in the distributed JAR are
included unmodified under src/generated/resources/ so that their editable
source is supplied too. These additions are identified in SOURCE-PROVENANCE.json.
The packaged source is not a claim that the original JAR was modified.

Rebuild
Use a JDK 21 installation and run sh gradlew --no-daemon jar in the source root.
The wrapper/build downloads its pinned build dependencies; neither runClient
nor runServer is necessary. The build used Gradle 8.14.3, NeoForge 21.1.228,
Create 6.0.10-280 and Azul JDK 21.0.13. Deprecated API/Gradle warnings were
emitted, but the build completed successfully. These upstream warnings were
not suppressed or repaired in the corresponding source.
