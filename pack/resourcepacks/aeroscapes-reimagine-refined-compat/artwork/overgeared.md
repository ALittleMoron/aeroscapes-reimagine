# Overgeared held models

The models and pixel materials are original deterministic composition with AI
assistance. They follow the silhouettes of the Overgeared 1.7.1 item icons and
the scale and rectangular shaft construction of the installed Refined Tools
3.0 iron tools. No Refined Tools geometry or artwork is copied. The resource
pack references the native Overgeared sprites for inventory, ground and frame
views. It applies the new meshes to first- and third-person held views.

The copper and steel tools follow their native silhouettes. Pickaxe heads use
a raised socket and tapered working ends. The hammers use a square striking
face, a narrower peen and a wooden handle. The iron and steel tongs have two
arms, a pivot and short jaws. Compact wooden tongs have a fixed heel, two spring
arms and inward-facing jaws. The held tool casts show their recessed wells and
pouring openings, and the knappable rock has stepped stone facets. [Hammers in
the Colonial Williamsburg collection](https://emuseum.colonialwilliamsburg.org/objects/67907/sledge-hammer) ·
[Iron tongs in the Museum Wales collection](https://museum.wales/collections/online/object/8b9284e3-a203-36b6-be16-5727bb20a120/Medieval-iron-tongs/)

Armor trims and worn layers stay with the provider. Arrows retain the native
tint and potion-state selectors. Paper templates, blueprints and unfinished
metal stock retain their native sprites.
